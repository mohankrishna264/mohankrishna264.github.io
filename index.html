    YuktiHire — AI Job Application Platform & Chrome Extension

    YuktiHire turns a job posting into a submitted application in under five minutes. A Chrome extension activates on any job portal, reads the job description, and produces a tailored resume, cover letter, and ATS match score — then fills the form.

    Objective

    Remove the repetitive work from job hunting: instead of rewriting a resume for every posting, generate a targeted one from the JD itself, score it against the role, and auto-fill the application without leaving the page.

    Highlights

    🔶 One-Click JD Parsing: Activates on LinkedIn, Workday, Greenhouse, and Indeed, extracting role requirements, skills, and keywords directly from the live posting.
    🔶 Tailored Document Generation: Produces a role-specific resume in DOCX and PDF plus a matching cover letter, generated by an LLM against the parsed job description.
    🔶 ATS Match Score: Returns a match score with gap-based fixes, showing exactly which requirements the resume misses and how to close them.
    🔶 Auto-Fill: Populates the application form on the portal, cutting per-application time from about 25 minutes to under 5.
    🔶 Companion Web Platform: Aggregates listings via public job-board APIs, with an in-browser resume editor and application tracker.
    🔶 Analytics Dashboard: Tracks roles applied to, documents generated, and response outcomes so users can see what's actually working.

    Conclusion

    YuktiHire is an end-to-end LLM product rather than a demo: extension, backend services, document generation, and analytics, shipped as one working system that people use daily.
